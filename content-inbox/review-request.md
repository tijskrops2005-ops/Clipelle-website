# Review request: starting points for customers

How to use: send a customer one or two of these openings (by e-mail or WhatsApp) and ask them to
finish the sentence in their own words. Only what the customer writes back may go on the site.
Copy their answer, unchanged, into `reviews.md` with their name, role, stars and the permission line.
Never fill in the brackets yourself.
